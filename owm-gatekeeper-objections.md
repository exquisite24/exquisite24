# Gatekeeper Objections: Practice List

**For:** Mark and G-Anne | **Campaign:** RIA Outreach (Playbook v2)

**Rule for every call:** never hang up with nothing. Leave with a **transfer**, a **callback time**, a **direct line**, or an **email**.

**Your mindset:** the gatekeeper protects the owner's time. Be polite, short and confident. You are not pitching to them. You are asking for two minutes with the owner.

**Before you dial (Playbook Section 3):** check GHL and the lead source. Know if you're calling a **firm owner** or an **individual advisor**, and the person's first name.

**Never say to a gatekeeper (Playbook Section 1):** anything about selling, buying, merging or recruiting, any numbers, or who else we're talking to. Every owner conversation is confidential.

---

## A. Screening questions

| # | They say | Goal | Coaching point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "What's this regarding?" | Give a short reason, then ask for the owner again. | One sentence only. Long answers sound like sales. |
| 2 | "Is this a sales call?" | Answer honestly, then give the real reason. | "No" alone sounds suspicious. Always say why you're calling. |
| 3 | "What company are you with? What do you do?" | Say who One Wealth Map is in one line. | Use the playbook line: a multi-family office with tax, estate, insurance and back office in-house. |
| 4 | "Does the owner know you? Are they expecting your call?" | Be honest and stay confident. | Never pretend to know the owner. |
| 5 | "How did you get this number?" | Give the real source. | Be honest. Confirm the wording with Sam first. |
| 6 | "Can I tell them what it's about?" | Share enough to be passed along. | "A confidential business matter" is enough. Save the details for the owner. |

## B. Owner not available

| # | They say | Goal | Coaching point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | "They're with a client." | Get a specific callback time. | "Later" isn't a time. Offer two options and log it in GHL. |
| 8 | "They're out of the office this week." | Find out when they're back. | Ask for a day and time, not just "next week." |
| 9 | "They're in meetings all day, every day." | Find a gap in their day. | Ask when their day starts or ends. Owners are often free early or late. |
| 10 | "They don't take calls. Everything goes through me." | Make the gatekeeper your partner. | Ask how important matters usually reach the owner, then follow that process. |

## C. Brush-offs

| # | They say | Goal | Coaching point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | "Send an email." | Get the owner's direct email, or a time to call. | Say yes, then ask for one more thing. Use the playbook email for the track. |
| 12 | "Send it to info@ and we'll pass it along." | Avoid the general inbox. | Politely ask for a direct address. If not, ask for a time. |
| 13 | "Leave a message and they'll call you back." | Leave a short message and set your own follow-up. | Don't wait for a callback. Ask when to try again. |
| 14 | "Just leave a voicemail." | Leave a voicemail under 20 seconds. | Use the playbook voicemail for the track. Say your number slowly, twice. |
| 15 | "We get calls like this all the time." | Show respect and stand out. | Agree with them, then keep the ask small: two minutes. |

## D. The gatekeeper decides

| # | They say | Goal | Coaching point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | "They're not interested." (said for the owner) | Let the owner decide. | Don't argue. Ask if you can send one short note. |
| 17 | "I handle these decisions." | Find out if they're an owner or staff. | Ask kindly about their role. Ownership matters need the owner. |
| 18 | "We already have an advisor." / "We don't need services." | Clear up the confusion. | They may think you're selling to their clients. Explain it's a conversation for the owner, from our founder. |
| 19 | "We're not hiring." / "They're not looking for a job." | Stay calm and keep it confidential. | Don't argue or explain the offer to the gatekeeper. Just ask for the right person. |
| 20 | "We're not selling the firm." | Protect confidentiality. | Never discuss selling with staff. The owner's plans may be private. |

## E. Rules and restrictions

| # | They say | Goal | Coaching point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | "We don't accept unsolicited calls. Remove us." | Exit politely and mark Do Not Call in GHL. | A polite exit is a win. It protects One Wealth Map's name. |
| 22 | "Calls are recorded. They can't discuss outside business on this line." | Respect the rule and find another way. | Don't fight compliance. Ask for an email or a better number. |
| 23 | "Our compliance team has to approve this." | Learn their process. | Ask where to send information. Following it shows professionalism. |
| 24 | "We're with a large broker-dealer. They can't take these calls." | Stay respectful and ask for another channel. | Never comment on their agreements. That's for the principals. |

## G. Outside the box

| # | Situation | Goal | Coaching point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 29 | The gatekeeper is chatty and friendly. | Build rapport and learn the owner's schedule. | Ask their name and use it. A friendly gatekeeper is your best ally. |
| 30 | The gatekeeper is rude or hangs up. | Stay professional and try another day. | It's not personal. Log it and try a different time. |
| 31 | "Can you hold?" (long hold) | Keep the call. | Prepare your opening while waiting. If it's too long, ask for a callback time. |
| 32 | "Talk to our operations manager instead." | Decide if that person can help. | Be polite. Ownership matters need the owner, but they may help you reach them. |
| 33 | The gatekeeper wants all the details. | Stay short without sounding secretive. | Share one line. The rest is confidential, for the owner. |
| 34 | Third callback, same gatekeeper | Turn repetition into familiarity. | Use their name and mention the last call. Polite persistence builds trust. |
| 35 | The owner answers unexpectedly. | Switch to owner mode right away. | Don't freeze. Confirm their name, then go to the playbook opening. |

---

## Quick reminders

- **Use first names:** the owner's and the gatekeeper's.
- **Be short:** your reason fits in one sentence.
- **Be honest:** never pretend to know the owner or make up a reason.
- **Keep it confidential:** no deal talk, no numbers, no other firm names.
- **Always ask for one more thing:** a time, a direct line, or an email.
- **Log everything in GHL:** gatekeeper's name, best time, what worked.
- **Respect "remove us":** a polite exit is a professional win.
