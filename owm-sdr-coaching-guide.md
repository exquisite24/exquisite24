## 3. The basics to coach (fundamentals first)

### A. Know the call goal
One goal: a 20-minute call with Ken. Not to explain the offer, not to win an argument, not to qualify everything. If the advisor agrees to talk to Ken, the call was a success.

### B. Call flow (simple version)
1. **Opener:** who you are, why you are calling, Ken's name early.
   "I wanted to reach out personally because our founder, Ken Himmler, came across your practice and thought it would be great for the both of you to connect."
2. **Permission + one question:** "Can I ask you one quick question?" Then a growth question, for example: "Is your calendar consistently full of qualified prospects, or is your lead flow uneven?"
3. **Value line:** a short statement tied to their answer. "Our advisors get a steady flow of qualified conversations..."
4. **Ask for the meeting:** "Does it warrant a confidential 20-minute conversation with Ken to see if it's a fit?"
5. **Lock it in:** offer two specific times. Confirm email and direct number.
6. **Log it** in GHL and the tracker the same day.

### C. The numbers game
Bea's ask on 10/08: **more dials, less research.** Wrong number? Move on. Still do follow-ups on anyone who showed interest. Mark agreed to pause research to raise dials.

### D. Make the script your own
Bea's main coaching point: follow the playbook, but deliver it in your own words. Mark said he is "hesitant to freestyle." Coach him that personal delivery is not breaking the script. The facts and the goal stay the same; the words can sound like him.

## 4. Objection practice (for the 10/09 session)

These are the objections the SDRs named on 10/08. The "Try this" lines are drafts based on Bea's suggestions. Check with Bea and Playbook 2 before the SDRs use them.

| Objection | What they say now | Try this |
|---|---|---|
| **"We're good / we're not a fit."** (Mark's most common) | "What made you think you're not a fit?" and "No obligation, just advisor to advisor." | Acknowledge first: "That makes sense, it sounds like the practice is doing well." Then: "That's actually why Ken wanted to connect. He talks with firm owners who are already growing, about where they want to take it next. Would 20 minutes be worth it just to compare notes?" |
| **"What is this for? / Are you selling something?"** | "We want to see if we can complement what you're doing." (Mark feels it sounds vague.) | Keep "complement." Add a clear purpose and redirect: "We're connecting with firm owners to speak with our founder, Ken Himmler. It's a chance to exchange perspectives on where your practice is heading and any ways to grow it further. Quick question so I don't waste your time: ..." |
| **"What is the meeting with Ken about?"** | Mark said he does not know how to answer. | "Ken will share what's worked for OWM's growth and hear about your goals. If there's a fit, he'll walk you through it. If not, you still leave with a useful conversation." Do not describe recruiting or terms. |
| **"OWM looks small / my firm performs better."** | Mark said advisors see OWM as small. | Shift the focus to Ken: his years in the industry and his experience. (Get the real facts about Ken's background from Sam or the playbook. Do not invent them.) |
| **Gatekeeper asks "What is it for?"** | Often ends the call or goes to voicemail/email. | Be brief and confident: "It's [name] calling for [advisor] on behalf of Ken Himmler, our founder. It's a short business conversation between advisors. Is [advisor] available, or what's the best time to reach them directly?" |

**Gatekeepers and compliance:** G-Anne thinks advisors avoid talking on recorded business lines. Bea's view: other regulated industries (for example, legal) still get through, so execution matters more. A fair middle ground: always ask the gatekeeper for the best direct line or best time, and log direct numbers when Apollo provides them.

## 6. Suggested daily coaching session (30 minutes)

1. **Check-in (3 min):** "Kumusta? How many dials yesterday, and any conversations with advisors?"
2. **Review one real call (7 min):** listen to one recording. Ask: "What went well? What would you say differently?"
3. **Drill one objection (12 min):** role play the same objection 3 times. Arque or Bea plays the advisor. Make it harder each round.
4. **Set today's commitment (5 min):** dials target, follow-ups, and one thing to try. Use their own words. On 10/08 Mark committed to booking 1 to 2 the next day; G-Anne committed to at least 1 per day.
5. **Close (3 min):** remind them to log calls in GHL and the tracker, and to message on WhatsApp if anything comes up.
