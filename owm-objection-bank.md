## Part 2. Rules every answer must follow (from Playbook v2, Section 1)

1. No valuations, prices, multiples, payout or deal terms, ever.
2. Approved figures only: about $300M, about 40% growth.
3. Never imply that clients automatically come over in a deal.
4. Recruits: never ask for client lists, never comment on non-competes or non-solicits.
5. Never say who else OWM is talking to.
6. When stuck: **"Great question. That's exactly what [Principal] will cover. Let me get you time with them."**

**The formula for any objection:** Acknowledge → Answer briefly or redirect → Ask a question or ask for the meeting.

## Part 4. Brainstorm session plan (about 45 minutes)

1. **Warm-up (5 min):** each SDR gives the 60-second multi-family office answer, no notes (playbook readiness check 1).
2. **Sort objections (10 min):** ask each SDR which 3 objections from Part 3 they have actually heard. Start with those.
3. **Write their own versions (15 min):** for each objection, they write one line in their own words, following the formula. This is Bea's "make the script your own" point.
4. **Role play (10 min):** Arque or Bea plays the advisor. Rotate: gatekeeper, owner, individual advisor.
5. **Wrap (5 min):** list the lines that need Sam's approval (B4, C4, C5, G5 and any new ones). Send them to Sam in one message.

**Open questions to send Sam:**
1. Can SDRs state the call purpose plainly, as the playbook opener does?
2. Approved wording for minimums, based on Ken's comment.
3. Approved facts about Ken beyond "built and sold a few firms."
4. Who runs recruit calls, and who sends the NDA?
5. Does G-Anne use the same Playbook v2 as Mark?
