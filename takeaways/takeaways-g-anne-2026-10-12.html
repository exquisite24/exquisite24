Coaching takeaways: G-Anne

Coaching session, October 12, 2026 · One Wealth Map RIA outreach · 2-minute read

Key realization: The playbook is your guide, not a script to read. With gatekeepers, say less and sound sure. With prospects, ask first, then use their answer.

Stop

  • Pitching to the gatekeeper.No AI, leads or services. Save it for the owner.
  • Saying "business matter" again and again.
  • Stopping at "No, she's not expecting my call."Always add the reason and ask again.
  • Pitching right after "I'm not interested."Ask a question instead.
  • Words that invite a no:"if you're interested," "would you be open to hearing about," "is it worth..."
  • Using the old playbook questions, like "What's the biggest thing holding back your growth?"
  • Saying "regarding with."Just say "regarding."

Continue

  • Asking "Can I ask you a quick question?" while they're still on the line.
  • Being polite and thanking the gatekeeper.
  • Getting more assertive each round. Keep that energy.
  • Going for a specific day when they say "next week."

Start

  • First name only, then ask:"Hi, this is Gladys from One Wealth Map. I'm looking for [Owner]. Are they available right now?"
  • Say "our founder, Ken Himmler," not just "Ken."So nobody asks "Who's Ken?"
  • Ask the v2 routing question before you pitch.Like a good doctor: ask questions first, then give the right medicine.
  • Get their direct number the first time they say "I have to go."
  • Before each call: breathe, smile, and keep a steady pace.The first 20 seconds set the tone.

Lines to keep on your screen

Gatekeeper, "What's this regarding?" "It's about [Owner]'s advisory practice and some longer-term business plans. Our founder, Ken Himmler, would like to talk with [Owner] directly. Is [Owner] available right now?"

Gatekeeper, "Is she expecting your call?" "No, she isn't expecting my call. I'm calling on behalf of our founder, Ken Himmler. He'd like to talk with her about the practice. Is she available right now?"

Prospect, "Not interested." "I understand. May I ask, what are you focused on right now with your practice? Are you keeping what you've built, or looking to grow?"

Prospect, "I have to go." "Of course. Before you go, what's the best number to reach you directly?"

Every answer: Answer their question, add one line of context, then ask a question. Only say what's true, and only use approved facts.
