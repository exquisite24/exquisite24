Coaching Takeaways

The goal: get past the gatekeeper, ask before you pitch, and leave every call with a booked time.

STOP

  • Pitching to gatekeepers. No AI, leads or services.
  • Repeating "business matter."
  • Stopping at "No, they're not expecting my call."
  • Asking permission: "Do you have two minutes?" "Can I ask you a quick question?"
  • Easy-no phrases: "If you're interested," "Would it be worth...," "Would you be open to..."
  • Pitching right after "Not interested."
  • Negative questions: "What isn't working?" "What's holding back your growth?"
  • Vague lines: "This is an outreach call." "I want to evaluate..."
  • Saying Ken knows them, asked for them, or wants to meet them.

CONTINUE

  • Confident, warm tone.
  • Listening, then adapting to what they say.
  • Staying polite and persistent with gatekeepers.
  • Confirming date, time, time zone and email before you hang up.
  • Sending payout and money questions to the team.

START

  • Answer, add one line, then make a request.
  • Ask the routing question before you pitch.
  • Use their answer: "That's exactly why I'm calling."
  • Close with two specific times.
  • Get a direct number before they hang up.

ACTION ITEMS

Before you dial

  • Check the exact name in GHL.
  • Keep your talking points on screen.
  • Breathe. Smile. Steady pace.

On every call

  • Use your first name only.
  • Gatekeeper: end with "Could you put me through, please?"
  • Prospect: ask before you pitch.
  • Close with two times.
  • Get a direct line.

After every call

  • "Take me off your list" = Do Not Call in GHL right away.
  • Log the call and update your tracker.
  • Write down one line that worked.

LINES TO USE

Gatekeeper: "What's this regarding?""It's about [Owner]'s advisory practice. I'm calling on behalf of our founder, Ken Himmler, and it's best discussed with [Owner] directly. Could you put me through, please? Thank you."

Gatekeeper: "Are they expecting your call?""No, they're not. I'm calling on behalf of our founder, Ken Himmler, about [Owner]'s practice. Could you put me through, please?"

Prospect: "Not interested.""I understand. What are you focused on right now with your practice? Keeping what you've built, or growing it?"

Prospect: "I'm good.""That's great to hear. Out of curiosity, how many more clients could you take on right now?"

Close"Are you free [day] at [time], or would [time] work better? I'll send the invite and call you 5 minutes before. What's your best direct number?"

Every answer: answer, add one line, then a request or open question. Only say what's true.
