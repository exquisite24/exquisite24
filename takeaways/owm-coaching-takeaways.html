Coaching Takeaways

The goal: get through the gatekeeper, ask before you pitch, and end every call with a booked time.

STOP

  • Pitching to gatekeepers. Save AI, leads and services for the prospect.
  • Repeating "business matter." Say the real reason once: "It's about [Prospect]'s practice."
  • Asking for permission. "Do you have two minutes?" and "Can I ask a quick question?" make it easy to say no.
  • "If you're interested" or "Would it be worth..." Same problem. They invite a no.
  • Pitching after "Not interested." Ask what they're focused on instead.
  • Saying Ken knows them, asked for them, or wants to meet them. It isn't true, and client reviews flag it.

CONTINUE

  • Your confident, friendly tone.
  • Listening first, then answering what they actually said.
  • Staying polite when a gatekeeper pushes back.
  • Repeating the date, time, time zone and email before you hang up.
  • Passing money and payout questions to the team.

START

  • Gatekeeper: answer in one line, then ask to be put through.
  • Prospect: ask a question before you pitch.
  • Use their answer: "That's exactly why I'm calling."
  • Offer two specific times. Not "When are you free?"
  • Get a direct number before they can hang up.

BEFORE YOU DIAL

  • Check the exact name in GHL.
  • Keep page 2 open.
  • Take a breath. Slow down your first sentence.

ON EVERY CALL

  • First name only.
  • End with a request, not a yes-or-no question.
  • Pushback? Find the line on page 2.

AFTER EVERY CALL

  • Opt-out? Mark Do Not Call in GHL right away.
  • Log the call and update your tracker.
  • Share one line that worked in this chat.

LINES TO USE

Gatekeeper

"What's this regarding?"It's about [Prospect]'s practice. I'm calling on behalf of our founder, Ken Himmler. Could you put me through, please?

"Are they expecting your call?"No. It's about [Prospect]'s practice, and it's best discussed with them directly. Could you put me through, please?

"Can you tell me more?"It's about [Prospect]'s practice and some longer-term plans. That's a conversation for [Prospect]. Could you put me through, please?

"Is this a sales call?"It's not a sales pitch. It's about [Prospect]'s practice. Could you put me through, please?

"They're in a meeting."No problem. You have their calendar. What time today is best to reach them?

Prospect

OpeningHi [Prospect], this is [Name] from One Wealth Map. Thanks for taking my call. I'll be quick. Then go straight to your question.

"Not interested."Fair enough. Just so I understand, what are you focused on with your practice right now? Keeping what you've built, or growing it?

"I'm good."Good to hear. Do you still have room for more clients, or are you at capacity?

"What's in it for me?"Fair question. Our founder has built and sold a few firms, so he's been on both sides of these decisions. The call is to see where you want your practice to go and what options fit.

"I have to go."Of course. What's your best direct number? I'll call you back tomorrow at [time].

The close[Day] at [time] or [time], which works better for you? I'll send the invite now and call you 5 minutes before.

Every answer: answer, add one line, then a request or open question. Only say what's true.
