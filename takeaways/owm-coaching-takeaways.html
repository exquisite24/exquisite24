Coaching Takeaways

The goal: get past the gatekeeper, ask before you pitch, and leave every call with a booked time.

STOP

  • Pitching to gatekeepers. No AI, leads or services.
  • Repeating "business matter."
  • Stopping at "No, they're not expecting my call."
  • Asking permission: "Do you have two minutes?" "Can I ask you a quick question?"
  • Easy-no words: "If you're interested," "Would it be worth...," "Would you be open to..."
  • Pitching right after "Not interested."
  • Negative questions: "What isn't working?"
  • Saying Ken knows them, asked for them, or wants to meet them.

CONTINUE

  • Confident, warm tone.
  • Listening, then adapting to what they say.
  • Polite persistence with gatekeepers.
  • Confirming date, time, time zone and email.
  • Sending payout and money questions to the team.

START

  • Answer, add one line, then make a request.
  • Ask before you pitch.
  • Use their answer: "That's exactly why I'm calling."
  • Close with two specific times.
  • Get a direct number early.

BEFORE YOU DIAL

  • Check the exact name in GHL.
  • Talking points on screen.
  • Breathe. Smile. Steady pace.

ON EVERY CALL

  • First name only.
  • Gatekeeper: end with a request.
  • Prospect: ask before you pitch.
  • Close with two times.

AFTER EVERY CALL

  • Opt-out = Do Not Call in GHL now.
  • Log the call. Update your tracker.
  • Note one line that worked.

LINES TO USE

Gatekeeper

"What's this regarding?"It's about [Owner]'s practice. I'm calling on behalf of our founder, Ken Himmler. Could you put me through, please?

"Are they expecting your call?"No. It's about [Owner]'s practice, and it's best discussed with them directly. Could you put me through, please?

"Can you tell me more?"It's about [Owner]'s practice and some longer-term plans. That's a conversation for [Owner]. Could you put me through, please?

"Is this a sales call?"It's not a sales pitch. It's about [Owner]'s practice. Could you put me through, please?

"They're in a meeting."No problem. You have their calendar. What time today is best to reach them?

Prospect

"What's in it for me?"Fair question. Our founder has built and sold a few firms, so he's been on both sides of these decisions. The call is to see where you want your practice to go and what options fit.

"I have to go."Of course. What's your best direct number? I'll call you back tomorrow at [time].

The close[Day] at [time] or [time], which works better for you? I'll send the invite now and call you 5 minutes before.
