Coaching Recap | 10/13 Eastern Time

GENERAL OBSERVATIONS

  • Gatekeepers are still the main challenge. Keep going. Every call is practice.
  • Listen, then respond. When a gatekeeper says "wrong office" or "they're busy," answer that first. Don't go back to the script line.
  • Clarify before you give up. Confirm the company and ask for the prospect by first name.
  • Share less, ask more. Mention Ken only if the gatekeeper asks. Ask for the best time to call back.
  • Email is the last resort. Try for a callback time or a transfer first, unless they ask for an email.
  • Understand the playbook, don't just memorize it. When you know why each line is said, you can say it your own way.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

  • Acknowledge first. Respond to what they said, then go back to your goal.
  • Be direct. Answer the question, add one line, then make your request.
  • Callback time over email.
  • Same meaning, your words. The playbook is a guide. Say it in a way that feels natural to you.
  • Always honest. Only say what's true. Use approved facts only.
  • Energy matters. Smile, breathe and keep a steady pace. Your voice is your tool.
