With Bea (Client Success Manager) and Arque (Success Coach)

Where we stand: Most calls today ended at the gatekeeper. More dials alone won't change that. A sharper first 20 seconds will.

GENERAL OBSERVATIONS

  • You know the script. Now own it. Prospects can hear when you're reading. One told us "you're too vague." That was the script talking, not you.
  • Every gatekeeper answer is a clue. "Wrong office" means ask for the right number. "She's busy" means ask when she's free. Treat it as a door, not a wall.
  • Every extra word is a reason to say no. Say less at the front desk. No pitch, no AI, and mention Ken only if they ask.
  • An email is easy to ignore. A callback time isn't. Go for a transfer or a time first. Send an email only when they ask for it.
  • Your voice is all they get. Energy, pace and a smile come through the phone. Low energy sounds like low confidence.

KEY TAKEAWAYS: 5 RULES FOR THIS WEEK

  1. Listen first. Answer what they said, then go back to your goal.
  2. Ask, don't tell. Questions get you through. Pitches get you hung up on.
  3. Time beats email. Leave every call with a time, a number or a transfer.
  4. Your words, same meaning. The playbook is a guide, not a script to recite.
  5. Truth only. Nothing you say should need explaining later.

  • Say "Do you have two minutes?" zero times.
  • Post one line that worked in the group chat every day.
